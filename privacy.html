Privacy Policy

for the iOS apps Fold & Focus, Klappkarten, Sofortbild, Peilung and Portalwacht · Last updated: October 2026

In short

The apps do not collect any personal data. There is no account of our own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The apps do not connect to our own servers or to any third-party servers. Everything you create in the apps stays on your device. The only exception: at your request, Portalwacht can use Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and online matches (see below).

What the apps store on your device

  • Fold & Focus: focus sessions, tasks, history and settings. Widgets and Live Activities read this data through shared storage on the device.
  • Klappkarten: decks, cards, review history and settings. Imported files (CSV, .apkg) are only read on the device. Reminders are local notifications; there is no push server.
  • Sofortbild: the camera is only active while the camera screen is open. Photos are stored only inside the app. When you tap, you can share a photo or save it to your photo library; for this the app only asks for permission to add photos, not to read them.

The apps never send this data off your device. It may be included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have enabled that in iOS; Apple's terms apply. Deleting an app also deletes its data.

Permissions

Portalwacht: the game stores your progress (stars, skill tree, best scores and a game in progress) only on the device. There are no ads.

Game Center (Portalwacht, optional): leaderboards and online matches use Apple's Game Center. Game Center is only used when you open a leaderboard or want to play online, and it requires your Game Center account with Apple. Apple processes your Game Center player name, your scores and the game data of an online match (inputs such as towers built) under its own responsibility, see Apple's privacy policy. Other players see your Game Center name depending on your settings. We do not receive or store this data. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in the iOS settings.

Nearby co-op (Portalwacht): for nearby co-op, two devices connect directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (local network), without internet and without a server. Only game inputs and a random name such as “Commander 123” are transmitted, not your device name. iOS asks for the “Local Network” permission for this.

Duel by message (Portalwacht): a duel link contains only the map, the seed, the planned attack waves, optionally a name you choose and, when sent back, your result. You send the link yourself through an app of your choice; we are not involved.

Location (Peilung): Peilung asks for your location while the app is open to calculate the direction and distance to places. The calculation happens only on your device; your location is neither stored nor sent. Places you save are kept only inside the app. Without permission, the app shows a notice and no directions.

Location (Peilung), camera (Sofortbild), adding to the photo library (Sofortbild) notifications (Klappkarten, Fold & Focus) and local network (Portalwacht) are only requested when you use the feature. You can revoke them at any time in iOS Settings.

App Store

The apps are distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes data under its own responsibility when you download and update apps, see Apple's Privacy Policy. At most, we receive aggregated, anonymous statistics from Apple if you have agreed to share them with developers.
